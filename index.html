Our Story

Built on Faith, Driven by Purpose

Caring Hearts wasn't born in a boardroom — it was born from a journey of transformation, resilience, and an unshakable desire to give back.

Our founder, Juan, knows what it means to start over. After walking through the hardest seasons of life — facing adversity head-on and coming out the other side — he discovered something powerful: the greatest gift we can give another person is genuine, heartfelt care.

That conviction became Caring Hearts. Every caregiver we hire, every family we serve, every plan we create is infused with that same spirit — the belief that every person, at every stage of life, deserves to be seen, heard, and truly cared for.

We are not a corporation. We are a family — and we want yours to feel that from the very first call.

Faith-centered, people-first approach
Transparent, honest communication with families
Personalized care plans — never one-size-fits-all
Ongoing staff training & professional development
